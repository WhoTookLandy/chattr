Auto-hiding bottom navigation bar on Home

NEW
• Auto-hiding bottom tab bar on Home: Scrolling down on Home (Live or Following) smoothly hides the bottom navigation bar so more of the feed is visible; scrolling back up or returning to the top brings it right back.
• Mini player bottom clearance: When the audio-only pill or floating video mini player is active on Home, it smoothly glides down closer to the bottom edge while the tab bar is hidden and lifts back up when the tab bar reappears.

PLEASE TEST
1. Scroll down and back up on both Home → Live and Home → Following — confirm the bottom tab bar hides cleanly on downward scroll, reappears on upward scroll or at the top, and doesn't flicker near the bottom edge.
2. Play a stream in Audio-Only or mini player mode, return to Home, and scroll down/up — confirm the floating mini player smoothly tracks the tab bar's clearance.
3. Switch between Live/Following, open Settings from the top-right avatar, or switch tabs while hidden — confirm the tab bar always restores cleanly.
