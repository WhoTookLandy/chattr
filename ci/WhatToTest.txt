Watch while you chat

NEW
• Streams play right inside chat. Tap the video for controls, the title, tags and Subscribe; swipe down on it to hide it.
• Mini player: back out of a chat and the stream keeps playing in a floating player across the app. Tap it to return, × or fling it down to stop, drag it to another corner.
• Audio only (🎧 on the video, or ⋯ → Audio Only): chat takes the whole screen while the stream keeps playing as audio.
• Native video player (You → Video, experimental): Apple's player with picture-in-picture, fullscreen and a max-quality setting. If it can't load a stream, chattr switches to Twitch's web player.
• Jump between open chats from the "1 / N" chip on the video. Close a chat from the ⋯ menu.
• Home → Live now lists every live channel you follow (no more "See all").
• Followers-only, slow mode and other chat restrictions now show in the message box.
