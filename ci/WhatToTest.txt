More room for chat in Audio only and Picture in Picture

IMPROVED
• Audio only no longer shows a bar under the channel name. That bar repeated the name and viewer count, so chat now starts right under the top bar.
• Pause and Show video are now in the top-right corner, opposite the back button. Touch and hold them for Stop Listening.
• The channel name at the top shows a headphones icon in Audio only, and a picture-in-picture icon while the stream is in Picture in Picture.
• Picture in Picture works the same way: no bar, and a "show video here" button in the top-right corner.

PLEASE TEST
1. Open a live channel with the video showing, tap the video, then tap the headphones button. The video should disappear, chat should fill the screen and the audio should keep playing.
2. Use the top-right pause button: the audio should pause and resume, and the icon should switch between pause and play.
3. Tap the video button in the top-right corner: the video should come back and the top-right buttons should go away.
4. In Audio only, touch and hold the top-right buttons and choose Stop Listening: the audio should stop and the stream card should collapse.
5. Swipe down on the video to start Picture in Picture: chat should fill the screen with a "show video here" button in the top-right corner. Tap it and the video should return to the chat.
6. While listening, swipe to another live chat: the audio should switch to that channel and the top-right buttons should stay. Swipe to an offline chat: the buttons should go away.
