Faster and smoother

IMPROVED
• Busy chats use less CPU: new messages are drawn in slightly larger batches when chat is moving fast, so scrolling and typing stay smooth during raids and hype moments. Quiet chats are unchanged.
• Animated emotes appear straight away when they've been seen before, with no blank flash, and use less battery while they play.
• Images are stored on disk once instead of twice, so "Clear cache" in Settings now clears everything.
• Swiping between chats, opening or closing a chat and switching tabs do less work behind the scenes.
• Faster start: your follows load once instead of twice, global emotes and badges load together, and background emote downloads wait until your chat has loaded.

PLEASE TEST
1. Open a very busy channel and scroll back, then tap "Latest": chat should keep up and stay smooth.
2. Send a message in a busy chat: it should appear immediately.
3. Look at animated emotes in chat and in the emote picker, at a couple of text sizes (Settings → Appearance). They should animate at their normal speed.
4. Swipe between several open chats, then leave a chat with a stream playing: the mini player should appear as before (and hand off to Picture in Picture if that's on).
5. Quit and reopen the app: your last chat should resume, and the Following list should fill in.
6. Settings → Accounts & Data → Clear cache, then browse chat: emotes should reload normally.
