Watch while you chat

NEW
• Replies look cleaner: a thin connector into a one-line quote, no grey box.
• Video controls are plain icons again. Swipe down on the video for picture in picture.
• Tap the viewer count on the video for the viewer list (moderators only; sign out and back in once to grant the new permission).
• A compact Subscribe button next to the stream info (hold it for Gift a Sub).
• New tab bar: Home, Chats, Activity, Search. Chats has its own tab (with a red badge when someone mentions you). Settings live behind your avatar on Home.
• Home: swipe between Live and Following. Following is split into Live and Offline.
• Chats: live chats first, swipe left to close, and the top-right menu closes all offline chats at once.
• Long-press any channel for a preview with Watch, Listen (audio only) and Chat only.
• Search shows who you follow that is live (by category) before you type, and your followed channels first as you type.
• Streams play right inside chat. Tap the video for controls, the title, tags and Subscribe; swipe down on it to hide it.
• Mini player: back out of a chat and the stream keeps playing in a floating player across the app. Tap it to return, × or fling it down to stop, drag it to another corner.
• Audio only (headphones button on the video, or More → Audio Only): chat takes the whole screen while the stream keeps playing as audio.
• Native video player (You → Video, experimental): Apple's player with picture-in-picture, fullscreen and a max-quality setting. If it can't load a stream, chattr switches to Twitch's web player.
• Jump between open chats from the "1 / N" chip on the video. Close a chat from the + menu next to the message box.
• Home → Live now lists every live channel you follow (no more "See all").
• Tap any chat message to see that user's card: badges, join date, bio and their recent messages (drag it up). Reply, block or report from there.
• Message bar: press Return to send, and tap the + for more options. Tap the chat to put the keyboard away.
• Bots look like everyone else again (no more greyed-out messages). Compact bots is still an option in Filters.
• Followers-only, slow mode and other chat restrictions now show in the message box.

FIXED
• The Live / Offline bar no longer sticks to the top of lists.
• The app freezing after tapping back from a chat (for real this time).
