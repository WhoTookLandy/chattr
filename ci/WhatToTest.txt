TestFlight diagnostics, persistent logs, and live chat HUD

NEW
• Persistent in-app logs: Debug and Info logs now stay available in TestFlight without Xcode attached, and the previous session's log is preserved across restarts/crashes.
• One-tap diagnostic export: Settings > About > Developer > Recent Logs (and Copy Diagnostic Report) now lets you search logs, inspect a Live Chat Snapshot, and copy or share a full report including device/layout info, chat state, and UICollectionView geometry.
• Live Chat Debug HUD: Toggle "Chat Debug HUD" in Settings > About > Developer (or inside chat's ... > Developer menu) to show a compact live overlay of transcript branch, attach state, message counts, and list bounds/cells.
• TestFlight auto-unlock: The Developer section under Settings > About is now shown automatically in TestFlight builds.
