Native Low Latency mode & live latency telemetry

NEW
• Low Latency toggle (Settings → Video & Device → Low Latency): Tightens native AVPlayer's live-edge offset (6s → 2s) and forward buffer cap (auto → 2s) for native video, audio-only, and pinned background audio, and applies immediately to active streams.
• Live playback latency HUD: When Settings → About → Developer → Chat Debug HUD is enabled, a 3rd line shows live stream metrics (mode, LL/std, estimated e2e latency from HLS program date, live-edge lag, forward buffer, configured vs AVPlayer recommended offset, and stall count).
• Structured playback logs: Native player start, audio/video swap commits, and stalls now log latency and buffer telemetry in Recent Logs and exported Diagnostic Reports.
