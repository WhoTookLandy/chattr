Multi-audio background streams & Audio Mixer

NEW
• Pinned background audio (up to 3 streams): Keep up to 3 live Twitch streams playing in background audio while watching another channel's video, swiping across chats, or locking your screen.
• Audio Mixer sheet: Independent volume sliders for every native stream, 1-tap Solo, Mute All, promote-to-video, demote primary video to pinned audio, and "+ Add from Open Chats".
• In-video pinned audio bar: Slim chip strip right under inline video (tap chip to mute/unmute, × to unpin, sliders for mixer) — plus a configurable Floating Pill option in Settings → Video & Device → Background Audio Bar (landscape always uses the compact floating pill).
• Quick pinning everywhere: Toggle background audio directly from the chat header popover switcher, the video overlay headphones menu, swipe-right on open chats, or long-press channel preview menus.
• Multi-stream mini player & Lock Screen: Primary video stays corner-snapped above the unified Liquid Glass audio dock, and Lock Screen Play/Pause controls all active background streams together.
