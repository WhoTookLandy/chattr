Watch while you chat

NEW
• Settings are reorganised into Appearance, Chat, Video & Device, Accounts & Data and About, with search at the top.
• Sign in with a code: tap Approve on Twitch (or enter the code at twitch.tv/activate). "Use browser instead" is still there.
• Landscape: turn the phone sideways while a stream plays for chat beside (or over) the video. Drag the divider to resize chat. Options in Settings → Video & Device → Landscape.
• Create Clip from the ⋯ menu while a channel is live. Clip links in chat now play inside the app.
• Polls, predictions and Hype Trains appear as banners at the top of chat on your own channel (Twitch only shares them with the broadcaster). Toggles in Settings → Chat → Filters.
• Moderators: one-tap timeouts on the user card (1m, 10m, 1h, 1d; edit them from the shield menu), plus Ban, Warn and Unban.
• If you're timed out, banned or warned, a notice at the top of chat says so (with a countdown) and the message box explains why you can't send.
• Settings → Video & Device: Picture in Picture (With Sound On / Always / Off), When a Chat Opens (as last left / closed / autoplay muted on Wi-Fi), PiP When Leaving Chat, Keep Screen Awake and Haptic Feedback.
• Mini player: drag it anywhere and it settles in the nearest corner; pinch to resize.
• @ mentions: type @ and pick a name from the suggestions.
• Signed-out "just look around" chat now shows emotes and name colors.
• The keyboard goes away after you send, and the button left of the message box is now ⋯ (More).

Sign out and back in once to grant the new permissions (moderation, clips, polls and predictions).

FIXED
• Being signed out out of the blue when Twitch briefly rejected a request: token refreshes now always go through chattr's sign-in server.
• Tapping the "Latest" pill sometimes tapped the message underneath it.
• The Live / Offline bar no longer sticks to the top of lists.
