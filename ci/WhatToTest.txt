Chats side by side on foldable iPhones and iPad

NEW
• On an unfolded iPhone or an iPad, the Chats tab now shows your open chats on the left and the chat on the right. Tap a chat in the list to switch; the one you're in is highlighted.
• The tab bar stays available while a chat is open there, so Home, Activity and Search are one tap away.
• Folded, and on every other iPhone, Chats works exactly as before.

PLEASE TEST
1. On an unfolded iPhone (or an iPad), open the Chats tab with a few chats open. You should see the list on the left and "No chat selected" on the right.
2. Tap a chat: it should open on the right and its row should be highlighted. Tap another to switch.
3. Swipe left and right in the chat: it should move between chats, and the highlight in the list should follow.
4. Type a message: the layout should not jump while the keyboard is up.
5. With a chat open, fold the phone: the same chat should stay open full screen. Unfold it again: the list should come back beside it.
6. On a regular or Max iPhone, turn to landscape in a chat with video: the landscape video layout should look the same as before.
